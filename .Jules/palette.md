## 2026-06-17 - Consistent Focus States
**Learning:** Custom interactive components (like GlassButton, BottomNav, and SidebarNav) often lack standard keyboard focus states, hindering accessibility.
**Action:** Use standard Tailwind focus utilities (`focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-ring focus-visible:ring-offset-2`) across all interactive components to ensure a consistent and visible focus indicator.
