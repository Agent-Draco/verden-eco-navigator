## 2026-06-04 - Accessible Navigation Polish
**Learning:** Adding `aria-current="page"` explicitly and a `focus-visible` ring on custom tabbed navigation buttons (like `SidebarNav` and `BottomNav`) drastically improves accessibility for screen readers and keyboard navigation without negatively affecting mouse/touch users' experience.
**Action:** Continue applying explicit ARIA state management and `focus-visible` utilities to custom routing buttons or tabs that don't natively act as anchor links.
